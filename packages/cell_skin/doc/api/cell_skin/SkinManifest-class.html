SkinManifest

SkinManifest class final

The system's discovery document, representing the complete external "DNA."

In the framework's biological metaphor, SkinManifest is the Somatic Index. It is a comprehensive catalog of every Somatic Address Record (SkinCellDescriptor) physically exposed at the Skin Membrane.

When to use

  • Discovery Impulse: Served in response to a manifestRequest to inform AI agents, dashboards, and remote axons of the system's available capabilities.
  • Initialization: Used by RemoteCellClient to understand the Somatic Blueprints (schemas) of a distant target.

How it works

  1. Index Assembly: The describe method walks the reactive graph.
  2. Pore Mapping: It correlates cells in the graph with the SkinBindingTable to identify open pores.
  3. Blueprint Resolution: It extracts or induces the JsonSchema for every function to build a complete capability map.

Non‑obvious behavior

  • DNA Stability: The manifest is immutable once the membrane is open. The SkinBindingTable seals at open(), ensuring the Somatic Index remains a stable source of truth.
  • Root Prioritization: The manifest orders cells based on their registration in the CellGraph, ensuring "Root Cells" appear first in discovery.

See also

  • SkinCellDescriptor: The per-cell records contained in the index.
  • SkinBinding: The source of truth for exposure and blueprints.
  • SkinRouter: The junction that handles the manifestRequest.
