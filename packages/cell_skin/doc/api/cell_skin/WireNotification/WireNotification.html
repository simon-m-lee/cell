Creates a new WireNotification envelope.

In the framework's biological metaphor, this constructor assembles a Transient Impulse. It prepares a volatile signal that is intended to cross the Skin Membrane without triggering a Somatic State Transition or entering the Forensic Ledger.

When to use

  • Heartbeats: Use this to simulate or send "keep-alive" signals between the system and an external client.
  • Testing: Use this to verify that the Skin Membrane correctly handles and routes non-somatic, fire-and-forget impulses.

How it works

The constructor bundles a method name (the intent) and optional params (the nutrients) into a stable record. Because these impulses are transient, no correlation IDs or sequence numbers are required.

Parameters

  • method: The identifier for the impulse (e.g., 'ping', 'progress').
  • params: The Transient Nutrients (data) carried by the signal. Defaults to an empty map if no data is provided.

Non‑obvious behavior

  • Zero Trace: Objects created with this constructor are never ledgered. Once they are delivered to a listener at the membrane, they are effectively forgotten by the system.
  • Immutability: Like all wire messages, once the notification is constructed, its contents cannot be changed, ensuring the impulse remains stable during transport.

See also

  • WireNotification.method: The type of impulse being created.
  • WireNotification.params: The data payload of the impulse.
