AuditTrailController

AuditTrailController class

Holds the audit history, applies filters/search/sort, and pages the visible window for large ledgers.

The controller accepts receipts through three equally valid paths:

  • a List<ApplyReceipt> passed to the constructor,
  • a Stream<ApplyReceipt> of live receipts,
  • addReceipt / addOutcome calls from your apply pipeline.

Rejections are not in the ledger. ApplyRejected outcomes passed to addOutcome are stored separately as RejectionEvents, which AuditTrailView optionally interleaves into the timeline.
