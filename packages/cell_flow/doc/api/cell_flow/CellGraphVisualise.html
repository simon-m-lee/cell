CellGraphVisualise

CellGraphVisualise extension

Visualisation for CellGraph and Ledger.

When to use

The visualisation tooling ships two modes for each:

  • cells — the graph's topology. Nodes are cells; edges are bind/Synapses links. This is the structural view: "what depends on what".
  • deltas — the ledger's causal chain. Nodes are deltas; edges are pulse order. This is the forensic view: "what changed, in what order".

The two views answer different questions and both are useful. A forensic audit typically starts with deltas (what happened) and cross‑references against cells (who could have made it happen).

See also:

  • CellGraphVisualise — renders a graph's topology.
  • LedgerVisualise — renders a ledger's causal chain.
