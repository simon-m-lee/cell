LedgerEntry

LedgerEntry class

A single, immutable row in the ledger.

The row is the atomic unit of persistence: append writes it atomically, verify checks its hash against the previous row, and entriesSince reads them sequentially. Its toJson form is what FileLedgerStore writes (one JSON object per line) and what a future network store would transmit.

Non-obvious: pulseDigest is a digest of the whole pulse chain

Not just the pulse's payload. Two pulses that share a payload but differ in parent, trace, or context are different causal entities; the ledger records both, distinguished by their digests. This is what lets the replay engine reconstruct the pulse without re-storing the chain, and it is why PulseCodec.toJson captures the parent's digest rather than the parent itself.
